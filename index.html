Simulator Usage Guidance

Topsoil Turmoil is the result of a School Project from Georgian College, by a group of 3 students. This is a simulator of that finished project.

Click any tile to see it's current art/wording (Art by @Occastral). The developer text mostly matches the final product, but is sometimes worded differently.

Use the Start (X) Player game buttons in the top left to begin a round. The buttons will fully reset the deck(s) with the configuration provided.

To use the Shovel, you will need to disable Safe Mode (checkbox, top left). Safe Mode simply prevents you from placing one tile onto an occupied space. If you later want to place over the shovel, you will need to do the same.

When an Objective card is won, un-check it based on it's number from the Active Objective Cards list. This will prevent it from being assigned once more after being won.

Assigned Objective Cards full text, and number, will appear when a player's hand is hovered by the hosts mouse. To disable this, use the Hide Objective Cards on Hover toggle.

Remember, each player needs to secretly know their goals to play properly. Give players time to view their goals, and record their contents if needed.

When playing online, a host will need to put in all the moves, while non-hosts will need to see the screen to make their moves. I recommend Discord Streams (and ensure you share screen, not application, as popups do not show on application streams). True multiplayer is likely out of scope for this project.

The active player will have their hand marked with a red box border.

At any time, the host can hover the objective cards at the top of the screen to check their effects.

Use the Rulebook pdf button to open the rules in a new tab.

Revel in the Turmoil. May the best player win. You Grow Girl!
